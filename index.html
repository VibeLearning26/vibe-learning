The Challenge

Why DoubtHub?

Traditional communication methods scattered discussions and left critical doubts unresolved. We built a unified hub centered around the doubt itself.

Traditional Chat Apps

  • Messages Get Lost Doubts quickly get buried under casual conversations and text clutter.
  • Delayed & Unreliable Support No structural prioritization; questions depend on random notification luck.
  • Scattered Solutions Crucial answers are scattered across links, PDFs, and hundreds of chat history pages.

The DoubtHub Solution

  • Structured Topic Rooms Every doubt is structured into its own discussion thread that never gets lost.
  • AI & Real-Time Peer Collaboration Instant help from peers combined with automated contextual suggestions.
  • Central Knowledge Base Solved doubts are archived into a neat, searchable central directory for reuse.

Workflow

How It Works

A streamlined four-step path to solving doubts and building collaborative understanding.

1

Create or Join Group

Establish a specialized channel with friends or join public rooms based on topics you are currently exploring.

2

Post a Doubt

Input your query with screenshots, snippets, or voice notes. Tag appropriate subjects to alert target subject experts.

3

Members Discuss

Collaborators jump into the real-time nested thread, working together via inline notes and dynamic diagrams.

4

Get the Solution

Approve the best answer to lock in the solution. The thread is neatly catalogued for future study reference.

Future Scope

Our long-term architectural goal is to expand DoubtHub into a complete automated AI co-learning suite.

Phase 1

AI Answers

Deploy custom-trained retrieval augmented models to automatically draft contextually verified starting answers.

Phase 1

Voice Rooms

Integrate seamless spatial voice discussion huddles with real-time screen share canvas boards directly inside discussion cards.

Phase 2

File Syncing

Enable localized repository uploads, automatic workspace document linking, and full file permission security protocols.

Phase 2

Mobile Suite
